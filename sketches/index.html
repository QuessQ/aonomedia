Gallery Noir

A darkened gallery: near-black canvas, ivory serif type, one work per wall. The video hero plays full-bleed out of darkness.

Atelier Light

Warm porcelain background, italic serif headlines, asymmetric editorial grid. The video begins letterboxed and expands to full-bleed as you scroll.

  • Staggered parallax portfolio grid

Monolith

Fixed black rail with logo, nav, and a live section index; content scrolls beside it. Oversized type rides over the scrubbing video.

  • Most modern / avant-garde structure

  • Strong agency-grade identity presence
